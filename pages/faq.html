FAQ

How does local auth work?

It's stored in your browser using localStorage (no server). Your data is saved only in your browser.

Is my data private?

LocalStorage data is private to your browser profile. Do not use this for sensitive information in production.

How accurate is the calorie calculator?

It uses standard formulas (Mifflin–St Jeor) to estimate needs — use as a guideline and consult a professional for personalised plans.
