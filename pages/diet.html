    Diet Tips

    • What: Balance protein, carbs and fats — aim for a modest calorie deficit (~300-500 kcal/day) to lose weight safely.
    • Why: Manage hunger and preserve muscle mass by prioritising protein and fibre-rich foods.
    • How much: Protein target: ~1.2–1.8 g/kg depending on goal; fats: ~20–30% calories; rest from carbs.
    • How: Use simple swaps (whole grains, lean proteins, more veg), plan 2–3 batch-cook sessions per week, track portions for 2 weeks to learn patterns.
