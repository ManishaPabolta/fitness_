Who we are — Fitness Science

Platform mission

To make evidence-based fitness accessible for everyone — using clear training plans, nutrition guidance and community support so users form lasting healthy habits.

Build strength. Consistency • Science • Community

Short brand story: A small team of trainers and developers built this platform to bridge the gap between sports science and everyday routines. Our vision combines fitness and sports to empower individuals and local clubs.

Problem statement: Many people lack structured, evidence-based guidance and trackable progress — this platform aims to simplify that by providing plans, tracking, and community support in one place.

Target audience: beginners, active adults, athletes, coaches and local sports clubs seeking structured programs and easy tracking.

Core philosophy
Sustainable progress through small, consistent steps and data-informed programming.

FAQs

How is my data stored?

Your data remains in your browser LocalStorage. This prototype does not sync to a server.

Can I use this for medical advice?

No. Consult a qualified medical professional for personalised advice.

How accurate are calorie estimates?

They are estimates based on standard formulas (Mifflin–St Jeor and METs). Use them as a guide.
